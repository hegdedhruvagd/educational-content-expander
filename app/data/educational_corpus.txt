Plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make food through photosynthesis. Chlorophyll in leaves captures light energy. The plant releases oxygen into the air.
The water cycle moves water around Earth. The Sun warms water in rivers and oceans, causing evaporation. Water vapor cools into clouds and returns as rain.
An ecosystem includes living things and the places where they live. Producers store energy in food, animals eat plants or other animals, and decomposers return nutrients to the soil.
Fractions describe equal parts of a whole. The numerator tells how many parts are counted, while the denominator tells how many equal parts make the whole.
Forces can change the motion of an object. A push or a pull may make an object start moving, stop, speed up, or change direction.
Reading a story means noticing its characters, setting, and events. Readers can use details in the text to explain a character's choices and predict what may happen next.
